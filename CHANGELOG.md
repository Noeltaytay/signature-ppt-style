# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-22

- Split the system into **fixed structure/decoration/animation** and
  **content-driven palette**.
- Added `references/animation-design.md` with the fixed Fade/click sequence.
- Added `references/palette-selection.md` for choosing colors from topic,
  industry, brand, and imagery.
- Added `references/decision-log.md` mapping the user's feedback to rules.
- Added five palette recipes: blue-gray example, corporate navy, academic warm,
  product dark, culture ink.
- Added `scripts/apply_palette.py` to recolor the structural templates without
  changing geometry.
- Added `scripts/check_style.py` for structure, image, notes, and animation-key
  checks.
- Added anonymized templates for cover, contents, chapter divider, content page,
  and closing.
- Added examples for business review, academic defense, product launch, and
  career portfolio.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-22

- Initial personal blue-gray editorial style skill.
- Fixed 15-page architecture, cover/TOC/dividers/content/closing.
- Added chapter divider and content frame templates.
